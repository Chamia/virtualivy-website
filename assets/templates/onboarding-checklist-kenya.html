New Employee Onboarding Checklist — Kenya

How to use: Replace every [bracketed field] with your details and delete guidance notes in italics. This is a general template aligned with Kenyan practice — for high-stakes matters, have it reviewed. Need it done for you? Talk to Virtual Ivy.

Before day one

  • ☐ Signed contract & job description on file
  • ☐ Collect KRA PIN, NSSF & SHA/SHIF numbers, bank details, ID copy
  • ☐ Register employee for PAYE, NSSF, SHIF & Housing Levy
  • ☐ Workstation, email & system access ready
  • ☐ Welcome message & first-day plan sent

Day one

  • ☐ Welcome & office tour
  • ☐ Issue employee handbook & key policies
  • ☐ Health & safety briefing
  • ☐ Introduce team & manager
  • ☐ Confirm role, goals & probation expectations

First week

  • ☐ Systems & tools training
  • ☐ Assign a buddy/mentor
  • ☐ Set 30/60/90-day objectives
  • ☐ Confirm payroll details captured correctly

First 90 days

  • ☐ Weekly check-ins
  • ☐ 30-day and 60-day feedback
  • ☐ Probation review & confirmation before end of probation
