Employer of Record

Hire in Kenya — without a local entity

If your organisation wants to hire employees in Kenya but doesn't have a registered local company, navigating employment law can feel impossible. Virtual Ivy acts as the Employer of Record — taking on the legal responsibility so you can hire confidently and compliantly.

Ideal for international NGOs, remote-first companies, and global teams expanding into Kenya.

How We Support You

  • We become the legal employer on behalf of your organisation
  • Draft and manage locally compliant employment contracts
  • Handle payroll processing and tax remittances (PAYE, NHIF, NSSF)
  • Ensure full compliance with Kenyan statutory requirements
  • Manage employee relations and HR queries day-to-day
  • Onboard and offboard employees in line with local law
  • Provide regular HR reporting to your global team
