FAQ

Questions, answered

Do small businesses need HR consulting?
Absolutely. Small and growing businesses often feel HR pain first, messy contracts, compliance risk, and no time to fix it. Outsourced HR gives you expert support without the cost of a full-time hire, so you can build proper foundations early.
What industries do you support?
We work across startups, NGOs and development organisations, SMEs, professional services, education, and technology companies, any growing organisation that needs reliable, practical people support.
Can Virtual Ivy assist with recruitment?
Yes. Recruitment is one of our core services. We run hiring end to end, job design, sourcing and headhunting, screening, structured interviews, and reference and background checks.
Do you provide HR compliance support?
Yes. We align your contracts, policies and processes with the Employment Act 2007 and statutory requirements, including PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Affordable Housing Levy.
How do we get started?
Book a free discovery call. We'll listen, assess where you stand, and recommend a practical plan tailored to your size and stage, no pressure, no jargon.
