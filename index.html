Client Stories

How we've helped

Representative examples of the work we do. We'll feature named client stories here with permission.

SME · HR Foundations
Challenge
A fast-growing business had no employment contracts, policies, or onboarding - just as hiring was about to accelerate.
What we did
Built a complete HR foundation: compliant contracts, an employee handbook, and a simple onboarding process the team could run themselves.
Result
People management went from ad-hoc and risky to structured and repeatable - without a full-time HR hire.
Recruitment · Talent Acquisition
Challenge
An organisation needed to fill several specialist roles quickly, but lacked the time and process to hire well.
What we did
Ran the search end-to-end - job design, sourcing, structured interviews, and background checks.
Result
The right people hired faster than the team could have managed alone, through a fairer, more consistent process.
NGO · Compliance & Policy
Challenge
A development organisation needed donor-ready HR policies and confidence that its employment practices were compliant.
What we did
Conducted an HR audit, refreshed policies, and aligned contracts and statutory practices with current requirements.
Result
Clear, compliant people systems that stand up to funder scrutiny - and far less day-to-day uncertainty.
