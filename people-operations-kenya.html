How outsourced people operations actually works

Bringing in outsourced HR support does not mean handing over a black box. We start with a short discovery: how your team is structured, what already exists (contracts, leave records, an HR system or just spreadsheets), and where the day-to-day pain is. From there we map a clear scope and a single point of contact so you always know who is handling what.

  1. Discovery and audit — we review existing contracts, records, leave balances and payroll inputs to find gaps before they become problems.
  2. Setup and cleanup — we organise employee files, standardise your onboarding flow, and put a simple, compliant records system in place.
  3. Run the day-to-day — onboarding new hires, maintaining records, tracking leave, supporting payroll, and being the first responder for routine employee queries.
  4. Report and review — regular check-ins so you see what is happening across your team and can plan ahead.

The goal of people operations Kenya support is simple: your HR administration runs quietly in the background while you focus on building the business.

The day-to-day HR backbone we keep compliant

Most HR risk in Kenya does not come from one big decision. It builds up quietly in the routine admin that gets skipped when everyone is busy. Solid HR administration Kenya means the basics are always in order: written contracts that reflect the actual role, accurate personnel files, and a clean trail for anything that could later be questioned.

  • Records and contracts — every employee with a proper written contract and an up-to-date file, in line with the Employment Act 2007's requirement for clear written particulars of employment.
  • Leave management — annual, sick, maternity and paternity leave tracked correctly so balances and statutory minimums are never guesswork. See our guide to leave entitlements in Kenya.
  • Payroll support — accurate inputs and statutory deductions handled in coordination with your payroll, PAYE, NSSF and SHIF obligations.
  • Policy backbone — consistent application of your HR policies so similar situations are handled the same way every time.

Keeping this day-to-day HR tight is the difference between an audit being a non-event and a stressful scramble.

In-house HR or outsourced people operations?

For many growing organisations, a full in-house HR department is more than the workload justifies, yet leaving HR admin to a founder, office manager or finance lead creates its own risks. Outsourcing the operational layer is often the practical middle ground.

  • A full-time HR hire makes sense once you have the headcount and complexity to keep one busy and the budget to match. Below that, capacity often sits idle while you still carry the salary.
  • Doing it off the side of a desk is cheap until something goes wrong — a disputed termination, a missed statutory deadline, or records that cannot be found when they are needed.
  • Outsourced people operations gives you experienced HR administration without a permanent salary, scaling up as you grow and down when things are quiet.

Many of our clients use this as a stepping stone, leaning on HR outsourcing until the team is large enough to justify their own HR function — at which point we help hand everything over cleanly.
