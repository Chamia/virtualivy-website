How our talent acquisition process works in Kenya

Strong hiring is a system, not a scramble. Our talent acquisition Kenya process is built around a recruitment pipeline you can see and trust, so a critical role never depends on whichever CVs happen to land in the inbox that week. We start by understanding the business problem behind the vacancy, then build outward from there.

  1. Role and scorecard definition. We agree on what success looks like in the first 12 months, the must-have competencies, and the realistic market for the role. Clear job descriptions and scorecards prevent the moving-goalposts problem that derails so many searches.
  2. Market and talent mapping. We map where the right people actually sit, including those who are not actively job-hunting, and benchmark expectations on pay and seniority.
  3. Active outreach and pipeline build. We approach, engage and qualify candidates directly rather than waiting for applications, keeping a live shortlist warm.
  4. Structured assessment. Competency interviews, practical exercises and reference checks applied consistently to every shortlisted candidate.
  5. Offer, close and onboarding handover. We help manage the offer conversation and notice-period risks, then hand over cleanly so the new hire starts well.

Because the work is documented at each step, you keep the pipeline even if a single candidate falls through, which is the core difference between a managed search and a one-off advert.

In-house TA vs outsourced executive search in Nairobi

Most growing organisations do not need a full internal talent function on day one. The honest question is which roles justify dedicated effort and which can be handled in-house. Building an internal team means salaries, tools, job-board subscriptions and management time, and it only pays off at consistent hiring volume.

Outsourced talent acquisition makes sense when hiring is occasional but high-stakes, when you need confidentiality, or when the role sits outside your usual network. For senior and leadership hires in particular, executive search Nairobi expertise gives you access to passive candidates and a discreet, market-mapped approach that internal teams rarely have the bandwidth for. Many of our clients run a hybrid model: they handle routine roles internally and bring us in for the searches where a mis-hire would be expensive. If your needs are more transactional, our broader recruitment services may be the better fit.

Why employer branding shapes who applies

The candidates you can attract are largely decided before any role is advertised. Employer branding is simply the reputation you carry as a place to work, expressed through how you describe roles, how quickly you respond, how candidates are treated during interviews, and what current employees say about you. Strong employer branding widens your pipeline and lowers the salary premium you need to compete on.

  • Consistency. A clear, honest story about the work, the team and the growth on offer, repeated across every touchpoint.
  • Candidate experience. Prompt communication and respectful interviews, because every candidate talks about how they were treated, whether hired or not.
  • Credibility. Realistic role expectations that match what new joiners actually experience, which protects retention.

We help you present your organisation well to the market without overpromising, so the people you attract are a genuine fit rather than a future regret.

What talent acquisition costs and how we scope it

There is no flat price for talent acquisition Kenya engagements, because a confidential executive search and a single specialist hire are very different pieces of work. We scope every engagement to your size, stage and the seniority of the role, then quote clearly after a free consultation, so you always know what you are paying for before you commit.

What shapes the scope:

  • Seniority and scarcity of the role, since harder-to-find skills need deeper market mapping.
  • Number of roles and whether it is a one-off search or an ongoing recruitment pipeline.
  • Depth of assessment required, from standard competency interviews to fuller leadership evaluation.
  • Confidentiality, where discreet, mapped outreach takes more dedicated effort than an open advert.

It helps to weigh fees against the cost of a bad hire, which includes wasted salary, lost productivity, re-hiring and the disruption a wrong senior appointment causes across a team. Hiring well the first time is almost always cheaper than fixing it later.

FAQ

Talent Acquisition in Kenya: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between talent acquisition and recruitment?
Recruitment fills a vacancy now; talent acquisition is the longer-term, strategic approach, building pipelines, employer brand and a repeatable hiring process so you consistently attract the right people.
Can you build our hiring process, not just hire for us?
Yes. We design and optimise recruitment processes, scorecards, interview structures, employer branding and sourcing strategy, so your hiring improves long after the engagement.
Do you do executive search and headhunting?
Yes. For senior and hard-to-fill roles we run discreet, proactive headhunting rather than relying only on applicants.
