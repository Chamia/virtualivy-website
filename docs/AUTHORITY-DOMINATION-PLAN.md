_Evidence-based. Goal: most authoritative HR consultancy in Kenya across Google + AI engines._
_Compiled 2026-07-03. Phase 1 (technical/entity/AEO) is complete and deployed._

## 0. The one insight that reframes everything

For commercial queries ("best HR firm Kenya", "HR consultant Nairobi"), **neither Google nor the AI engines rank company homepages. They rank and cite third-party directories, "best-of" listicles, and consensus sources.**

Evidence:
- Google SERP for the category is owned by GoodFirms, Clutch, BusinessList.co.ke, and blog listicles (Stellar HR "15 top recruiting firms", Bridge Talent "Top HR outsourcing companies", nairobionline).
- Perplexity: listicles = **50% Top-3 rate** and ~**32% of all citations**; commercial queries weight review platforms + third-party trust over the brand's own site. NAP-consistent presence in a directory/association/newspaper "carries far more weight than one that exists only on its own website."
- Across AI engines the **top 15 domains capture 68% of all citations** — almost none are company homepages.

**Implication:** Virtual Ivy's next win is NOT another on-site change. It is getting Virtual Ivy *into* the third-party graph (directories, listicles, the IHRM register, editorial pages) with consistent identity. That is where both Google and the LLMs look.

## 5. Assumptions challenged (you asked me to)
1. **"More schema / technical points"** → **STOP.** Confirmed diminishing returns; Phase 1 was the last worthwhile code layer. You were right.
2. **"Publish more blog articles"** → **Trap.** You already have 24+. More thin posts don't build authority. Shift from *volume* to a *few citation-worthy assets* (task 7/12) + third-party mentions.
3. **"Rank our homepage for 'best HR firm Kenya'"** → **Largely futile.** Those SERPs are directories/listicles. Getting *onto* them (task 5) beats trying to outrank them.
4. **"llms.txt will drive AI citations"** → **Weak evidence.** Keep it (cheap), but it is not a lever. Don't over-invest.
5. **Unconventional, evidence-backed:** prioritize **Perplexity** (22× more brand-friendly than ChatGPT) and **third-party listicles/directories** over any further on-site work; use the **#1-ranking LinkedIn page** as the branded-demand seed.
