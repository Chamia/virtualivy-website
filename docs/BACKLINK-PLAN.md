# Virtual Ivy Consultancy — Prioritized Backlink Plan

_Research-verified (56 domains checked) · Companion to [SEO-PLAYBOOK.md](SEO-PLAYBOOK.md). This supersedes the older "first 10 links" list in the playbook where they differ._

A practical, do-it-yourself plan. Work top to bottom. Use the **exact same NAP** everywhere (bottom of this doc). "(verify)" = confirm the detail yourself before relying on it.

**Link types, quick truth:**
- **Citations** (Google, LinkedIn, Facebook, directories) are usually **nofollow** — they don't pass much "link juice" but build local-SEO trust + NAP consistency. Do them anyway; they're the foundation.
- **Dofollow editorial links** (guest posts in news/HR sites) are the real ranking power — Section 3.

## 4. Industry memberships (paid — credibility + likely link; decide later)
Not "this week" (they cost money) but real, high-trust. Cite real membership numbers on the site only — never fabricate.
- **IHRM Kenya** (ihrm.or.ke/membership) — Kenya's statutory HR body; most credible. Has an "HR Firm" category.
- **Federation of Kenya Employers (FKE)** (membership.fke-kenya.org/register) — national employers' body; highly relevant.
- **ASSEK** (assek.ke) — Startup & SME Enablers; strong strategic fit. info@assek.ke for fees.
- **KEPSA / KNCCI** — broad business credibility (general, not HR-specific); higher cost.
- **Devex** org profile — relevant if pursuing NGO/development clients.
